The workflow

One prompt in. A reviewed campaign out.

Describe who you want to sell to in plain English. Reach finds matching companies, audits their web presence, scores each for opportunity and drafts outreach that references what it actually found. You review and send.

  1. 01Describe the customer
  2. 02Find companies
  3. 03Audit
  4. 04Score
  5. 05Draft outreach
  6. 06You review
  7. 07Send

Most outbound tools optimise for volume. Reach is built around precision: understanding each prospect's actual situation and writing to it, rather than merge-tagging a template. It grew out of the prospecting Interface does for its own business.
